# 1. Principal formal changes from Publication Candidate 4

1. Descriptive truth-satisfaction \(\vDash_D\) is separated from commitment realization \(\Vdash_R\).
2. The two registers are distinguished primarily by speech-act force, principal function, inferential role, and standard of assessment rather than by two exhaustive semantic species.
3. Fixed-base KLM consequence begins only after stable addressable content has been supplied; it does not solve the Frege–Geach problem of content constitution.
4. The practical taxonomy contains five verdicts: strong warrant, mere admissibility, exclusion, infeasibility, and underdetermination.
5. Classical commitment negation is an adopted modeling choice within the Boolean fragment.
6. Formal determinacy is model-relative and must be joined to affected-party standing, option-set integrity, independent challenge, noncompensation, reversibility, and repair.
