## Mandala

[Volume I: architectural argument](companions/mandala/Mandala_v0.4_Volume_I.md), [Volume II: relational atlas](companions/mandala/Mandala_v0.4_Volume_II.md), and the [interactive relationship graph](companions/mandala/Mandala_v0.4_Interactive_Graph.html) form an official noncontrolling integrative companion. The 64-stage traversal is not a universal chronology or proof by exhaustion. Pending relationships retain their declared status.

M&E and Foundations control shared definitions, methodological rules and registered wording. The domain papers control domain claims. The introduction, Denying Knowledge, Non-Egoic Artificial Mind and the Mandala have the bounded companion roles stated in their texts. Older versions and books remain historical or interpretive sources, not overriding authorities.
