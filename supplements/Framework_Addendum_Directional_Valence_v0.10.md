# Wave 5 Technical Addendum: Valence as Directional Evaluation

## Status

Conditional bridge hypothesis and research program. This addendum records the fuller rationale behind the condensed reader-facing §8.4 and CU-V8. It does not establish valence in any disputed system.

## Core distinction

A universal identity between valence and the gradient of one coherence functional remains retired. The retained hypothesis distinguishes:

- a **phasic** component associated with the direction and rate of change of a candidate subject's endogenous better–worse organization; and
- a **tonic** component associated with standing relative to protected margins.

The primary object is the vector of oriented margins and their realized rates. Any scalar intensity is optional, local, subject-relative, and unable to erase mixed direction.

## Information-geometric boundary

Where an endogenous variable is itself a CU-M14-licensed distribution, Fisher-Rao speed may quantify the magnitude of statistical motion. The metric has no better–worse sign: a curve and its reversal have the same speed. Direction remains supplied by the declared endogenous evaluation and protected margins.

## Discriminating program

1. Manipulate rate while holding level approximately fixed.
2. Manipulate level while holding rate approximately zero.
3. Test whether mixed affect tracks coordinate disagreement better than net scalar change.
4. Compare short- and long-horizon direction.
5. In artificial systems, test whether candidate variables are endogenous, globally integrated, interventionally causal, persistent, and tied to a candidate perspective.

## Failure conditions

Narrow or retire CU-V8 if rate manipulations add no explanatory or predictive value beyond positional models, if the phasic/tonic distinction fails to generalize, or if candidate progress signals prove explainable entirely as external objective implementation without a subject-relative organization.
