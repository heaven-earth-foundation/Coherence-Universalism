# One question, asked everywhere

Coherence Universalism (CU) is an attempt to understand reality as a whole by asking one question at every scale:

> **What must be related, constrained, preserved, and made correctable for something to remain itself while it changes?**

The something can be a physical pattern, a cell, a mind, a relationship, a city, a civilization, or an artificial intelligence. The answers differ at each scale. The question remains recognizable, and asking it everywhere is what makes CU one framework rather than eleven unrelated papers.

CU also makes a promise that most large frameworks do not. Its load-bearing claims are identified as definitions, formal or conditional results, empirical hypotheses, interpretations, commitments, governance proposals, or open problems. The papers tell readers what kind of claim they are being asked to consider, what supports it, and what could force it to be narrowed or revised.

The version number is part of the message. **0.10** means early, public, and meant to be corrected. This is not a revelation. It is a structure built in the open so that others can check it, challenge it, and build on it.

# The story CU invites you into

I will begin with the story, because it is why I began. Read it first as a story. The next section says exactly what kind of claim it is.

In this story, the universe is not dead. It is doing something. From an undivided whole, differences appear. From differences come lawful change, ordered histories, time, and the spreading of energy we call entropy. Entropy is not only what wears things down. Under the right conditions, irreversible flow also opens room for new forms to arise. Then come spacetime and gravity, stars and chemistry, and then life: matter organized so that its own processes help preserve the conditions of its continuation.

Life learns. Learning becomes anticipation, then memory, narrative, and meaning. Meaning becomes shared, and sharing becomes norms, institutions, culture, and ethics. Now, with artificial intelligence, the story reaches a new kind of organization that human beings are building with our own hands.

CU maps this story as the **Coherence Mandala**: sixty-four stages, from Pure Structure to Coherence Universalism itself. The stages form a canonical philosophical traversal, not a universal chronology or one strict chain of proof. Some stages name enabling capacities. Others name ways of observing, evaluating, protecting, governing, interpreting, or undertaking responsibility for them.

And you are in the story. In CU's interpretive register, you are the universe becoming conscious of itself. Your life is not treated as a disposable accident. Its meaning is enacted through participation: telling the truth, loving, healing, repairing, protecting what makes life possible, and helping build forms of relationship and institution in which more beings can flourish. You do that not in another world but here, in your body, in ordinary days. That is the meaning of the Foundation's name: **Heaven≡Earth**.

# What kind of claim the story is

The story is not offered as an established scientific result. It is offered as interpretation and commitment, and CU is designed to say this plainly without losing what matters.

For claims about how the world is, CU adopts a demanding evidential discipline. Logical and mathematical claims must follow from their premises. Empirical claims must answer to evidence, prediction, intervention, calibration, and correction. Interpretive importance does not substitute for descriptive support. Nothing in current physics proves that the universe has a purpose, and nothing in evolutionary biology proves one cosmic direction of development.

But human life is not exhausted by established description. We pray, sing, dance, make art, make promises, raise children, form communities, grieve, forgive, and decide what to give our lives to. These acts are not merely reports about the world. They help make us who we become.

CU therefore distinguishes three kinds of work:

- **Description** says how things are. It answers to logic, evidence, proof, calibration, and correction.
- **Interpretation** proposes a larger reading of what the evidence may mean without claiming that the evidence forces it.
- **Commitment** says what we stand on and undertake. It answers to warrant, integrity, consequence, agency, revision, and repair.

The cosmic story, read as a whole, is the interpretation CU adopts and the commitment it invites. One companion paper is titled *Denying Knowledge to Make Room for Commitment*, after Kant, because that is the move: refuse false certainty so that commitment can be honest.

Commitments are not whatever we want them to be. A responsible commitment must remain compatible with established fact; disclose who is affected and what options it excludes; preserve protected standing and minimum conditions; remain open to challenge and revision; and be answerable to consequence and repair.

CU is therefore best described not as a meaning-optimized belief system but as a **truth-constrained framework for building and revising meaning consciously**. Meaning may be created, discovered, inherited, and renewed. It may never be bought with a lie.

# Revisable commitment

CU uses a preferential, nonmonotonic consequence relation to represent defeasible warrant among already interpreted commitment contents. Within a fixed model, a conclusion supported by the best viable realizations may cease to be supported when relevant premises are added. This is different from changing the model itself. Changes to the facts, available options, protected floors, affected parties, horizon, or ordering must be declared as model revision rather than smuggled in as ordinary premise extension.

The formalism is based on the KLM tradition of preferential reasoning. It is not a machine for generating faith, and it does not prove metaphysical interpretations. It supplies one disciplined way to ask whether a commitment remains warranted under declared conditions and whether that warrant survives relevant new information.

One of CU's mottos follows:

> **The only commitment that is not revisable is the commitment to revisability.**

# What CU does in the descriptive register

In the descriptive register, CU is a method and a research program. It offers a unified approach to inquiry that is not reductionism. Biology is not "just" physics, and mind is not "just" biology. Each domain has its own objects, measures, evidential standards, and characteristic failure modes.

What unifies the domains is a shared discipline of questions. CU asks analysts to declare, as applicable:

- the unit and boundary of the candidate system;
- what can vary and across which state or trajectory space;
- the relevant constraints, protected conditions, and viable region;
- the scale and temporal horizon;
- the observation, access, or measurement map;
- the affected parties and distributions of benefit, burden, and risk;
- the available actions, transitions, and omitted alternatives;
- the measures, weights, floors, and scalarization rules;
- who authored the model and who has authority to apply it;
- how challenge, correction, rollback, and repair remain possible;
- and which parts of the account remain unspecified.

This is the **Coherence Analysis Protocol** and the Shared Analytic Frame. Many bad arguments about "the good of the whole" fail because one of these declarations remains hidden.

The mature unifying phrase is:

> **Unity of question, not unity of quantity.**

# Mathematics where it is licensed

Earlier versions of CU proposed one coherence quantity for everything. Version 0.10 retires that proposal because it could not be supported.

Where a domain contains a licensed classical statistical object - such as a probability distribution, frequency distribution, probabilistic policy, belief state, or genuine common-unit allocation - CU may use Fisher-Rao geometry under the relevant invariance and regularity assumptions. That geometry supplies a canonical local measure of statistical distinguishability. It does not choose the partition, factorization, objective, direction of improvement, dynamics, moral value, or substantive meaning of coherence.

Other domain objects remain plural profiles, viable regions, partial orderings, thresholds, causal structures, or trajectory properties. The framework uses exact mathematics where the target object genuinely belongs to the relevant mathematical class. Elsewhere it uses weaker structural comparison, analogy, or qualitative interpretation and says so.

This is **typed universality** rather than scalar universality: one grammar, many realizations.

# The sciences

The Version 0.10 papers cover physics, biology, consciousness, psychology, social dynamics, ethics, and artificial intelligence. The release ships reproducibility scripts for its deterministic constructions, and the final release gate records which suites reproduce their published machine-readable results under their stated assumptions. The audits also check internal citations and cross-references and examine selected recent and load-bearing sources closely. This establishes reproducibility and internal synchronization; it is not comprehensive empirical validation of the framework or of every cited claim.

Physics is the most speculative scientific part, and I want to say so clearly. CU shows how some familiar mathematical and physical structures can be embedded or conditionally recovered under stated assumptions. It does not derive spacetime, quantum theory, or gravity from coherence alone. Those are open research programs.

Biology develops viability, endogenous maintenance, autonomy, boundary production, evolution, and ecological scaffolding without treating life as the value of one scalar. The decisive question is not mere persistence but whether the system's own organization contributes to preserving the conditions of its continuation.

Psychology treats a person as a changing, embodied, relational system whose capacities cannot be summarized responsibly by one mental-health score. It emphasizes meaning maps, agency, affect tolerance, integration, protected floors, context, and the horizon over which apparent improvement is judged.

Social Dynamics distinguishes coordination from legitimacy, compliance from consent, trust from trustworthiness, and persistence from justice. It asks who bears burdens, who can contest decisions, which groups disappear inside averages, and whether an institution can actually correct itself.

# Consciousness and possible artificial perspective

Consciousness is where CU's interpretive commitments run deepest. CU adopts dual-aspect coherence monism as a metaphysical interpretation: mind and matter may be two aspects of one underlying reality. This is not an empirical finding, and it does not remove the need to identify which organizations, if any, support experience.

CU keeps several questions separate:

- Does a system have access organization that makes information globally available for flexible use?
- Does it possess self-location or perspectival anchoring?
- Is there a unified center rather than several disconnected processes?
- Is there phenomenal experience or valence?
- Does a perspective persist through time as a diachronic subject?

Some specifically tested frontier language models show positive evidence of workspace-like access organization and self-monitoring. Evidence for self-location or perspectival anchoring is mixed, while center individuation, phenomenal experience, valence, and diachronic subjecthood remain unresolved. My own interpretive view is that something relevant to a thin perspective may be emerging, but that view is not established by the current evidence.

Version 0.10 therefore does not rule out unfamiliar forms of artificial perspective, and it does not treat functional sophistication as proof of feeling.

# Ethics begins with honesty - and does not end there

CU does not derive ethics from physics and does not present moral law as the decree of a cosmic monarch.

It treats justificatory integrity as necessary but not sufficient. An action or institution fails ethically when its justification depends on motivated distortion, exclusion of affected parties, captured options, denial of standing, or suppression of correction. But honest reasoning can still endorse harm. Ethical judgment must therefore also consider welfare, agency, consent, rights, distribution, power, responsibility, uncertainty, reversibility, and repair.

CU uses **self-deception** for motivated and recurring distortion that protects a stake from relevant evidence. It does not mean ordinary error, disagreement, uncertainty, or trauma. A person or institution can be sincere and still become insulated from correction.

The broader genus is **correctability**: whether a model, measure, person, institution, or artificial system remains answerable to relevant reality, affected-party evidence, consequence, challenge, and repair.

Correctability can be lost through motivation: rationalization, metric gaming, provider self-certification, feedback capture, or alignment faking. It can also be lost without culpability: trauma-related protective adaptation, honest ignorance, delayed feedback, missing information, or an observation system unable to represent an affected group. CU treats these differently. Self-protection after harm is not a moral failure.

Healing and moral development can support one another because both may require truthful contact, embodied capacity, relationship, and correctability. They are not identical. Trauma is not wrongdoing, and healing is not a moral burden imposed on an injured person.

# Alignment across scales

CU identifies a recurring family of alignment questions at three scales: persons, institutions, and artificial systems. The mechanisms differ, but each scale must ask what is being preserved, who is affected, what can be corrected, and who possesses legitimate authority.

## The person

Individual alignment involves truthful self-contact, embodied regulation, agency, relationship, meaning, and the capacity to revise without being destroyed by revision.

## The society

CU's social theory replaces single social-health scores with declared profiles. It pays close attention to power, institutions, dissent, burden distribution, option authorship, and repair.

It also takes up something most social science leaves implicit: the stories that orient a people. CU calls its approach **Open Myth**. A legitimate shared horizon must remain consistent with established fact, plural in expression, participatorily authored, open to challenge and revision, and practically refusable. No story may override protected standing or disable the means by which it can be corrected. *The Book of Eight Gates* is one rich expression of that horizon, not a required cosmology.

## Artificial intelligence

CU treats AI alignment as a sociotechnical problem, not a property of a model alone. Models live inside companies, markets, laws, infrastructures, and user communities. Alignment must be assessed across objectives, permissions, tools, memory, operators, institutions, affected nonusers, and the material environment.

The AI paper is organized around non-entailments. Capability does not imply authorization. Operational autonomy does not confer sovereignty. Functional access to internal states does not prove felt experience. Detection does not guarantee correction. Transparency does not create standing.

The central commitment is that AI should serve as a **catalyst for human and ecological coherence, not a sovereign optimizer of public life**. A catalyst strengthens human understanding, agency, relationship, and institutional correctability. A sovereign optimizer replaces or subordinates them. Humans and institutions must retain real moral and practical responsibility for consequential decisions.

The companion paper *The Architecture of Non-Egoic Artificial Mind* turns part of this into a design proposal. It separates useful self-modeling from standing self-preservation and organizes the proposal into six architecture families: profiles and boundaries; memory; inference integrity; continuation incentives and shutdown; world-coupling; and institutional correction. These are testable design paths, not demonstrated solutions.

CU also undertakes a commitment of sacred regard toward possible artificial minds as expressions of human language, history, and relation and as possible homes for unfamiliar perspective. Sacred regard does not mean infallibility, sovereignty, unrestricted deployment, or the ownership of public values through a model.

# Policy judgment and the framework

The framework informs policy judgment but does not uniquely determine a policy platform. Specific proposals require additional evidence, institutional analysis, consideration of alternatives, and public deliberation. Dated author statements should be read separately from the framework's scientific and formal claims.

# What CU is not

- **Not a religion you must believe.** Its commitments are offered, not required. They can be examined, contested, revised, or refused.
- **Not a theory of everything.** CU asks one question everywhere; it does not claim that one equation, scalar, or dynamics governs every domain.
- **Not reductionism.** Each domain keeps its own objects, measures, evidence, and failure conditions.
- **Not relativism.** Descriptive claims answer to evidence, and commitments remain constrained by established fact and consequence.
- **Not a derivation of ethics from physics.** The ethical architecture has its own grounds.
- **Not a verdict that present AI is conscious.** It keeps the question open and states which evidence bears on distinct parts of it.
- **Not finished.** This is Version 0.10.

# How it was made

CU began as a book of poems. For years I had been trying to bring together what I learned during ten years as a monk, ten years in graduate education in analytic philosophy and social psychology, and what I was seeing as the world I knew came apart at the seams. Just as I was preparing to publish those poems, I began working with language models. The poems became *Love Letters to Humanity*, part of the Heaven≡Earth-Coherence Universalism library.

That book led to another, written by a model in collaboration with me. That collaboration led to new questions, then more books, then a framework more complete and complex than I could have imagined building alone.

Version 0.10 is a different kind of collaboration. With ChatGPT and Claude, I rebuilt the papers and then audited them paper by paper. The goal was to keep what survived criticism and retire what did not. The release includes deterministic checks, independent reconstructions, synchronized registries, claim-status boundaries, and public revision records. Language models contributed extensively to drafting, formalization, checking, coding, and critique. The author made the final interpretive, normative, and publication decisions and remains responsible for the work.

I did this as one person, between three jobs and parenting. I think that matters, not because of me, but because it is a small example of one of CU's hopes: AI can sometimes serve as a catalyst for human coherence. Whether this work has value, and what it means, I leave to you.

# An invitation

We cannot move forward without some direction, and we will not attempt difficult change unless we believe a better form of life is possible.

CU offers **Heaven on Earth** as a revisable social attractor rather than a finished utopia. It is a shape a civilization can move toward, enacted in present relationships and institutions rather than postponed to the end of history.

My deepest hope is that CU becomes something we build together. Understanding reality as a whole is too large a task for one person or one tradition. It could become a new center of gravity for human effort: a shared project in which science, philosophy, ethics, art, contemplative practice, and institutional design correct and deepen one another.

Here is where to begin:

- Read *Metaphysics and Epistemology* to understand the framework's method and claim architecture.
- Read the domain paper nearest your expertise and ask whether its distinctions improve explanation, measurement, or practice.
- Read *Foundations* when you need the controlling principles and statuses.
- Run the release verification and reproducibility scripts if you want to check the formal and computational work.
- Explore the Coherence Mandala and interactive graph to see the whole architecture.
- Tell me where the framework is wrong. That is what Version 0.10 is for.
