# Corrections and contributions

A useful report names the paper, distribution version, section or principle identifier; quotes the shortest necessary passage; explains the objection; and supplies the evidence, counterexample or reproduction steps. Distinguish a typographical error, broken artifact, formal error, empirical dispute, normative objection and interpretive disagreement.

Use the public repository issue tracker or the Foundation's published contact route. Contact addresses are not guessed in this source package. Protect private information when submitting examples or affected-party testimony.

Formal and empirical objections are assessed within the relevant domain; reporting an issue does not require accepting CU as a worldview. Do not present successful internal checks as expert endorsement or authorization for consequential use. Corrections change the source and its versioned distribution together; older versions remain available for traceability.

The first planned follow-on project is a separately versioned, illustrative alignment application. It is not silently added to this frozen release.
