# The Coherence Mandala v0.4 — Release-Convergence Edition

This package contains the synchronized two-volume Coherence Mandala companion set for Coherence Universalism v0.10 through RC3 Release-Convergence Wave 6.

   `Volume_I_The_Coherence_Mandala_Typed_Architecture_v0.4.pdf`

4. **Combined omnibus**  
   `The_Coherence_Mandala_Two_Volume_Omnibus_v0.4.pdf`

The interactive graph is a standalone local HTML file. Open it in a modern browser. Pending inherited relations are available but visually separated from adjudicated or newly defined relations.

## Publication status

The set is an **official but noncontrolling v0.10 companion**. M&E, Foundations, and the domain papers remain controlling for definitions, principle wording, claim status, and domain claims. The Mandala companion integrates and traverses those materials; it does not replace them.

## Synchronized architecture

The v0.4 registry contains:

- 389 active typed objects;
- 12 reusable shared services;
- 81 typed service-role invocations;
- 51 protection relations;
- 23 adjudicated strict prerequisites;
- 1,564 total relationship records.

- the correctability throughline, distinguishing motivated from non-culpable loss of correctability;
- the Statistical and Information-Geometric Representation Service under the revised CU-M14 licenses;
- the six-family Non-Egoic Artificial Mind architecture;
- Stage 64 as interpretive recognition plus commitment-register uptake, without descriptive entailment.

## Relationship status

The atlas is exhaustive relative to the current registries, not exhaustive relative to completed semantic adjudication. Every relationship record displays its type, audit status, evidence grade or warrant basis, and revision condition where available. Pending relations remain visible as candidates and do not enter strict proof order.

## Fascicles

Eight arc-specific atlas fascicles are provided in `atlas_fascicles/` as Markdown, DOCX, and PDF. A separate ZIP containing those fascicles accompanies this package.

## Technical layer

- `registries/` contains the machine-readable object, relation, service, protection, external-prerequisite, strict-dependency, and stage registries.
- `build/input/` preserves the generated v0.4 inputs.
- `build/rebuild_documents.py` rebuilds the DOCX editions from the reader-facing Markdown and figures.
- `build/verify_wave7.py` runs structural verification.
- `provenance/` records the frozen source-package hashes.
- `qa/` contains accessibility, verification, and final QA reports.

## Scope boundary

This publication reports the internal architectural results of the sixty-four-stage audit. It does not by itself validate CU in target-domain empirical data, prove that the sixty-four-stage traversal is necessary, or establish phenomenal, moral, physical, or metaphysical conclusions beyond the warrant assigned to each typed object and relation.
